faults cannot fire. The isolated observer cases fail before journal fault injection.

to restore contents of slots allocated while restoring a higher address.

fail and two pass. C# observes both affected addresses; four commit-rejection
cases fail and eight rollback cases already pass.

The fix prepares every observed undo record before journal I/O, marks incomplete
transactions rollback-only, attempts automatic rollback and retains failed cleanup
for retry. C# adds decorator.Rollback() for automatic cleanup without a handle.
