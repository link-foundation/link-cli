C# version rollback owner and admission (R1/R3/R8)

Before the fix, VersionControlDecorator clears _activeTransaction in its
rollback finally block even when the inner transaction remains rollback-only.
Owner Dispose also clears that reference before attempting rollback. The inner
transaction correctly keeps pending undo, but its version facade permits branch,
tag and checkout changes and cannot retry cleanup through owner disposal.

The unexpected success is vc.Branch("blocked") after failed rollback.

The matrix injects before/after undo, data Flush, rollback marker SetName and
journal Flush, through explicit transaction rollback, version-owner Dispose and
whole-graph Dispose. One-shot failures leave a real nonterminal inner transaction.

The version owner now retains its reference during Dispose and releases it from
rollback only when the inner transaction is committed or rolled back. Failed
undo/persistence preserves admission and retry. The terminal-state condition also
handles retention's failure after MarkRolledBack, without trapping the facade.

The same 24 cases verify branch/switch/checkout/tag/begin rejection, rejected
commit of rollback-only state, successful retry through the same entry point,
exclusive reopening of every owned file, exact two-row baseline/head recovery,
and a following committed/tagged version transaction. An additional real mapped
retention control leaves an older committed transition uncheckpointed, completes
a newer rollback, then fails retention's retry of the old checkpoint. The inner
transaction is terminal and version admission remains usable afterwards.
