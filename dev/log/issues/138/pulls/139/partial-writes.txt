Root causes:
  Rust/C# wrappers collect observed changes, but discard them on inner errors.
  Repeated callbacks additionally replace an initially absent before-state
  with an intermediate newly created row, preventing its eventual removal.

After:
  Record the observed partial changes before returning/rethrowing the error.
  Roll back automatic operations and release admission; leave explicit
  transactions owned by the caller with every change available for rollback.
  Preserve the original before-state across repeated callbacks.
  Every regression checks the baseline, following transactions and persistent
  reopening. At most three data rows are live in any fixture.

Scope: this verifies Result/exception failures with the storage callback contract.
It does not claim Rust panic safety or recoverability for silent mutations, and
journal/checkpoint failures retain their independent error semantics.
